Booking · what each one lets us style

Three ways to book, in our clothes.

Both open in a popup over the page. Everything outside the white window is ours in both cases. The difference is inside it.

Google appointment schedule

Our button, our backdrop, our window frame and close button. The booking page itself keeps Google's fonts, blue buttons and layout.

  • Styled here: button, backdrop, window, close button
  • Fixed: everything inside the window
  • No account change — this is the link we use today

Cal.com

Same shell, plus the booker and the confirmation screen take our colours, hairlines, corner radius and spacing through Cal's own variables. Its type stays Inter; its layout stays Cal's, and a free account shows a "Cal.com" mark under the booker.

  • Styled here: button, and the booker's colours, radius, spacing, light/dark, layout
  • Fixed: Cal's fonts and the arrangement of the booker
  • Needs a Cal.com account and the booking link moved

Cal.com, in a drawer

The same booker, this time as an inline embed inside a panel of ours that slides in from the right. The panel, its backdrop, heading, hairline and close button are plain CSS here, because the container is ours rather than Cal's.

  • Styled here: the whole panel, plus the booker's colours as in the popup
  • Fixed: the same Cal internals — fonts, arrangement, wording
  • Full screen under 640px, where a side panel has no room
